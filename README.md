# My Favorite Series: Dragon Ball Super

**Dragon Ball Super** is an action-packed anime series that continues the adventures of *Goku* and his friends as they defend Earth and face powerful warriors across multiple universes.

---

## Why I Recommend It
- High-energy battles and iconic transformations
- Great character growth for both **Goku** and **Vegeta**
- Expands the lore with new gods, angels, and parallel universes

### Top 3 Story Arcs
1. Tournament of Power Arc
2. Goku Black / Future Trunks Arc
3. Battle of Gods Arc
